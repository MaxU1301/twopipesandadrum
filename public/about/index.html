About

Two Pipes and a Drum is a Scottish Highland pipes and drum trio based in Southeast Michigan. We specialize in providing high-quality bagpipe and drum performances for a variety of events, including weddings, funerals, parades, and other special occasions. Our trio consists of two pipers and one drummer, providing a full and authentic sound that is sure to impress your guests.
